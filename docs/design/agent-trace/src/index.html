Four ways to see what the agents did

Each prototype is a different bet on the question you open it with. All four read the same real export of yesterday's Hermes runs () and show only counts, times, tool names and synthetic worker labels. Mark what you like or hate on each one; I will merge the winners.

Why omp's trace and stats feel intentional

  • Every page opens with one sentence of purpose and a strip of numbers that split the whole (wall = model + tool + idle, each as a share).
  • Defaults do the editing: idle gaps are compressed, deep tracks start collapsed, the newest sessions sort first, a 24h range is preselected.
  • One selection drives the canvas, the transcript and the drawer, and the keyboard contract is printed under the chart.
  • Numbers are mono and tabular, colour means one thing (model, tool, error), and failures are a badge in a column, not a colour wash.
  • It is honest about method: frustration says when it is regex versus a judge verdict.

What Arc's timeline adds (captured, not copied)

  • Replay as explanation: a 26 s clock, a square leading edge on the live span, 350 ms connector draws and a status line that settles into one summary.
  • Handoffs as elbows from the orchestrator, so parallelism and waiting read at a glance.
  • Pin a span and everything off its path drops to 28% opacity.
  • Where it falls short for us: it shows a 26 s demo, never hours, interruptions, stalls, cost against a quota or anything waiting on a person.
A

Event trace

What exactly happened, and where did it go wrong?
Emphasises
  • every tool call on one canvas, coloured by phase
  • failures, stalls, compressed idle
  • keyboard: J/K step, E next error, X next stall
  • sortable workers and tools with cost
Hides
  • replay and motion
  • runs start folded to one row each
  • anything you can't act on from a desk
Open questions
  • Is the day the right default, or the latest run?
  • Should errors and stalls be on by default or filtered?
  • Do you want the transcript text in Dash (private, local only)?
B

Run replay with state bands

Who did what, when, and who never came back?
Emphasises
  • one run, one row per worker, Hermes on top
  • dispatch and report elbows; interrupted ends in a cross
  • FSM band under each row (Dispatched, Running, Stalled, Verifying, DraftPR)
  • replay at 60x to 960x, scrub, pin to dim
Hides
  • individual calls inside a span
  • other runs (pick from the menu)
  • tokens per call: totals are spread by calls (≈)
Open questions
  • Is replay useful after the fact, or only live?
  • Should a resumed worker draw a bridge from the cut one?
  • Do your steering messages belong on the Hermes row?
C

Stats

Is the system healthy, on budget, and what is blocking it?
Emphasises
  • three headline numbers: Opus pace, workers cut, items waiting on you
  • burn vs even pace per lane
  • roles from ROLES-v0, with "no source" where we lack data
  • ranked bottlenecks with evidence
Hides
  • individual runs and calls
  • per-hour cost charts
  • frustration (needs your message text; off by design)
Open questions
  • Which bottleneck should Kerdoios act on by itself?
  • Should "verified" mean tests ran, or that a checker agent agreed?
  • Do you want deep-work blocks (calendar, ActivityWatch) on the attention chart?
D

Pocket

Do I need to do anything right now?
Emphasises
  • live workers with state and quiet time first
  • Needs you, batched by kind of decision
  • a dot per worker for the whole day
  • one-thumb dock, 44 px targets, bottom sheet per worker
Hides
  • timelines and tables
  • all tool names except the last one
  • controls (Steer, Stop, Resume) are shown but disabled
Open questions
  • Should this be Dash's home tab or a pull-down?
  • Which of Steer, Stop and Resume do you want first?
  • Is "quiet 4m" the right alarm, or a per-task deadline?
