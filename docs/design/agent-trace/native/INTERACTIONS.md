## What the trace views use

| Interaction | Source posts | Native implementation in Dash |
|---|---|---|
| Pinch-to-zoom around the focal point, pan with momentum, spring at the bounds | 8, 5 | RNGH `Gesture.Pinch` and `Gesture.Pan` run simultaneously. `withDecay` takes clamp and rubber-band. An over-zoom spring brings the scale back to 1–12×. |
| Long-press scrub with a playhead and haptic ticks | 5 (hover tooltip), 8 (playhead) | `Gesture.Pan().activateAfterLongPress(320)`. A tick fires every 15 min crossed (`haptic.select`) and on each hour (`haptic.tap`). The readout shows the clock and how many workers were live then. |
| Press states with a spring scale | 3, 4 | Rows use `PressScale` (SNAP spring, 0.985). Chips scale to 0.96. |
| Swipe chips: right = Do, left = Not now | 6, 3 | A horizontal `Gesture.Pan` with an underlay reveal. The threshold is 35% of the width or 800 px/s. One haptic on crossing the threshold and a success haptic on commit, then the chip collapses (layout transition). |
| READ strip animating in | 2 | Three variants: slide (fade-up, each line staggered), words (word-by-word reveal), and shimmer (a loader band, then the text settles). |
| Live items pulse subtly | 7, 8 | A looping opacity (0.55↔1) and scale (1↔1.35) on the live dot, plus a glow on the now-cap of live bars. |
| Shared-element-style expand from row to detail | 4, 8 | The row is measured with `measureInWindow`. An overlay springs from that rect to the sheet rect, the content fades in afterwards, and a drag down or back closes it. |
| Count-up numbers | 1, 7, 8 | `StatTile` tweens its value on mount and on change. |
| Reduced motion | all | `useReducedMotion()`. No decay, no pulse, and no count-up (the final value shows at once). Springs become instant and the expand becomes a crossfade. Haptics stay, because they are not motion. |

Not used now: drag-and-drop between columns (6), board/list morph (3), light/dark theme crossfade (Dash is dark only), and the collapsed "+N" summary bars (8). The last is a good next step for multi-worker rows.
