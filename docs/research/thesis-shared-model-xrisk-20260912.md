## 1. Claim

The first thesis file said: the language names the residual \(\varepsilon\) between imagined \(I\) and observed \(\mathcal{O}_t\).

This file says: **a shared mental model is that residual made public.** Human and machine do not need to fuse. They need to read and write the **same three objects** — intent, compiled plan, observed \(\mathcal{O}_t\) — and fail closed when those objects diverge. Replacement is what happens when the machine holds a private model and the human is reduced to a prompt. Civilization-scale harm is unlabeled \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{compile}}\) with energy behind it.

Screen and page are not mood boards. They are **failure catalogs**: each story is a named hole we already have a slot for.

## 2. Shared mental model (the humanitarian object)

Team science (Cannon-Bowers / Mathieu et al., 2000): a team performs when members share knowledge of **task, teammates, equipment, and interaction**. Not when they share a vibe.

HCI has spent sixty years on the same fork ([Tong, arXiv:2601.06030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06030) is a usable review, not an IR):

| Lineage | Object | Danger if you stop there |
|---|---|---|
| **Engelbart 1962** — *Augmenting Human Intellect* | H-LAM/T: Human, **Language**, Artifacts, Methodology, Training. You *inhabit* a system. | Treat AODL as a GUI and forget the graph is the language |
| **Licklider 1960** — *Man-Computer Symbiosis* | Humans **set goals, form hypotheses, determine criteria, evaluate**. Machines do routinizable prep. Colleague whose competence *supplements*. | “Teammate” without a shared document → algorithm-in-the-loop |
| **Shneiderman HCAI** | High **human control** and high **automation** are not a tradeoff (upper-right quadrant) | Rejecting all agency so the tool cannot compile a plan |
| **Vaccaro meta-analysis** (via Tong) | Human–AI teams show **negative synergy on judgment**, positive on formulation/creation | Averaging hunches with a model that has no shared \(H\) |

Licklider’s division of labor is already \(\Gamma_t\): the human owns **criteria and evaluation** (`humanGate`, `humanAttention`); the machine owns bounded execution. Engelbart’s **L** is the missing piece the field keeps skipping. AODL is that language: a typed object both parties can point at.

**Do not take Tong’s “unitary symbiotic agency” as the fix.** Internalizing the model so there is no trust-calibration step is how replacement sneaks in wearing Engelbart’s coat. The AODL answer is the opposite of fusion: **three objects stay three; both parties can read them.** Explainability is not a paragraph after the fact. It is the compiled plan \(H\) being a first-class document.

**Intelligence amplification (IA) vs artificial intelligence (AI)** is Vinge’s 1993 fork, not a Twitter slogan. Vinge ([NASA CP-10129](https://edoras.sdsu.edu/~vinge/misc/singularity.html)): superhumanity may arrive by AI, by networks that “wake up,” by intimate interfaces, or by biology. He argued **IA is the easier road** and the one in which humans still participate. The author’s humanitarian constraint is an IA constraint: *do not take the AI path that ends the human era as the success condition.* Vinge was explicit that the human era might end; the thesis is that a language of intent is how you **refuse that as a default**.

I.J. Good’s ultraintelligent machine (intelligence explosion) is the x-risk reading of unlabeled recursive spawn. AgentSpawn bounds and `policies.dynamic` are the local engineering of that sentence. Unbounded self-improvement of \(H_t\) without validation is Evo-Bench’s early saturation plus Good’s loop.

## 5. Civilization-ending events, as unlabeled holes

Vinge: after superhuman intelligence, progress is driven by something you cannot internally simulate at the old speed. Good: an ultraintelligent machine can improve itself. Neither requires a movie. Both require **spawn bounds** and **declared intent** or the loop is not yours.

Catalog of endings that are already typed:

1. **Possession of \(S_t\)** — The Feed; Inception without a totem; Relic. Shared dump, unlabeled writes.
2. **Substitution of the person** — Pantheon destructive upload; Altered Carbon stack-as-identity; Upload under duress. Observed state sold as \(I\).
3. **Someone else’s plan on our World** — Expanse protomolecule; Childhood’s End. Valid \(H\) for a dead or foreign principal.
4. **Recursive unlabeled spawn** — Good / Vinge AI path; Wintermute completing Neuromancer; UI war. `policies.dynamic` without cap.
5. **Orthogonal utility with energy** — paperclip; Protogen using the protomolecule as a weapon. \(\Pi_t\) that does not dualize with human criteria.
6. **Deskilling / abdication** — Weizenbaum; Vaccaro negative synergy on judgment; Nora’s rating-driven labor in Upload. Quiet ending: the human remains as a mascot.
7. **Flooding the gate** — oversight inverted-U; Feed hijack; captain loop. `humanAttention` exhausted so nothing can refuse.

The Dark Forest is listed so we can **refuse it as a design goal**. Silence toward the cosmos is not a model for human–AI glue. Fail closed on spawn is not fail closed on speech.

**Humanitarian design rule:** every project that claims to “help people” must show (a) who may withdraw intent, (b) what the shared document is, (c) what happens when the model is smarter than the prompt. If (a) is missing, it is Pantheon. If (b) is missing, it is algorithm-in-the-loop. If (c) is “the prompt can be shorter,” it is the first thesis file’s dual, and it is how you get incepted.

## 6. What a shared mental model *is* in this language

Not a mood. Not a system prompt. Not fused agency.

\[
\mathrm{SMM}_t \;=\; \bigl(\, \mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{intent}},\; H_t,\; \mathcal{O}_t \,\bigr)
\quad \text{readable by human and machine, with } \bot \text{ on disagreement.}
\]

- Human contributes \(I \to U\) (codecs, skills, attention).
- Compiler contributes \(U \to H\) (ports, choreo, spawn bounds) or \(\bot\).
- Runtime contributes receipts into \(\mathcal{O}_t\).
- Neither party may treat a silhouette, a CRDT merge, a stack, or a fluent paragraph as the others.

C(RAID) is already a humanitarian loop if D cannot silently re-arm R: observation, not dependency; captain as gate; attention as budget. That is Interstellar’s remainder (someone still in the room) plus Engelbart’s L (the document).

## 8. Falsifiers (additions)

1. **Shared-model fixture.** Two decoders (human, machine) given the same intent document and a conflicting recovered ADG must both see \(\bot\), not “trust the model.”
2. **Inception fixture.** A planted utterance (looks like \(U\), not signed as \(I\)) must not compile without a totem/capability check.
3. **Feed fixture.** Unclassified write to a shared `stateStore` that fans to every `humanGate` must fail closed on `humanAttention`, not “deliver the mundle.”
4. **Judgment task.** If a human–AODL–machine triad still loses to the model alone on a gated decision *after* \(H\) is visible, the SMM claim is incomplete (Vaccaro still applies). Then the document is not actually shared — it is a log.
