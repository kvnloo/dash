## Readings

In the STP reading, intent, plan, and observed are three sheaves over the same graph—not three names for one thing. Intent lives in the document: what the project is allowed to mean. Plan lives in the compiler profile: what may be built from that document under a chosen adapter. Observed lives in the event log and its receipts: what actually ran and what evidence backs it. Each layer carries only its own local data; they do not merge by assertion. Compatible observations may glue into one observed state; where gluing fails, the factory stops or a verifier may abduct—never an invented edge, and never a fourth object standing in for missing receipts.

Cousins (one line each, not kinds): \(\lambda_A\) is the intra-node calculus; Pact/Scribble/MPST check `message` as a session; AgentFlow recovers a dependency graph from framework source (audit, not authority); RLM treats the prompt as an object outside the window; AdaptOrch and Evo-Bench search over compiled \(\Pi\); oversight inverted-U is a human-attention budget already legal under `constraints.budgets`.

## Adapters

MCP is tools, A2A is peers, AG-UI is the user surface. LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen/MAF, Dify, and n8n are fragments or DAG runtimes. Hermes, Keel, Codex are this network. Visual \(\tau\) does not compile a chain-of-thought tree into `fanout`.
